Information we collect

PlainOS Audio Cleaner does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal data. The app does not use advertising identifiers, analytics services, or tracking tools.

Your audio and video

You choose media using Android's system file picker. The app reads the selected file only to provide the enhancement features you request. Audio analysis, noise removal, voice enhancement, previews, captions, and video processing are performed locally on your device. Your media is not sent to PlainOS or any third party.

Files stored on your device

The app may create temporary processing files and cached previews in its private app storage. Android normally removes private app data when you uninstall the app, and the app clears obsolete working files as part of its processing workflow. Finished files are saved only when you ask the app to export them.

Device information and diagnostics

Processing status, estimated completion time, memory use, GPU activity, power use, and error details may be shown on screen to help you understand and troubleshoot a task. These diagnostics remain on your device and are not transmitted to us.

Offline processing technology

The app includes audio and speech-processing components that run locally. They do not send your recordings to their developers or to online artificial-intelligence services. PlainOS Audio Cleaner is designed to work without an internet connection.

Permissions

The app uses only the access needed for features you choose, such as opening a media file and saving an enhanced copy. It does not request permission to browse your full media library when Android's file picker can provide access to a selected item.

Children's privacy

PlainOS Audio Cleaner is a general-purpose media utility and is not directed at children. Because no personal data is collected by the app, we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app's features or legal requirements change. The effective date above will be updated when a revised policy is published.

Contact

For privacy questions, use the contact details shown on the PlainOS Audio Cleaner Google Play listing or the contact section on joebains.com.
